About Zahra

Zahra has more than 15 years of experience as a physiotherapist. She holds a Master's in Physiotherapy from Shahid Beheshti University in Iran and a Master's in Kinesiology from Memorial University in Newfoundland.

She treats neuro-musculoskeletal conditions in people of all ages. Her approach is personal: a thorough assessment first, then a plan that combines manual therapy, dry needling and exercise.

Her passion is healthy aging, so people can keep doing what they love. She keeps studying, most recently in pelvic floor health, dry needling and resistance training for older adults.

Pelvic floor physiotherapy

Pelvic floor problems can cause pain and make daily life harder. They are common, they are treatable, and you do not have to put up with them.

Zahra's pelvic floor care is private, unhurried and respectful. It also matters beyond the pelvis: it can help with low back pain and overall quality of life.
