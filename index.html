About Zahra

Zahra is a registered physiotherapist with clinical experience dating to 2007 and Canadian practice since 2023. Her current focus is pelvic health, orthopedic conditions and persistent pain.

She works with a biopsychosocial approach, combining individualized exercise, manual therapy and education so you can understand your symptoms and manage them yourself.

Before moving to Canada she founded and ran a physiotherapy clinic and led a rehabilitation department in Tehran. That experience shapes how she works with other health professionals and plans care.

How Zahra helps

  • Assessment and reassessmentA thorough look at how you move and where it hurts, checked again as you progress.
  • Therapeutic exercisePrograms built for you, with home exercises you can keep using.
  • Manual therapy and myofascial releaseHands-on techniques to ease pain and restore movement.
  • Functional dry needlingFor muscle pain and tightness that will not settle.
  • Education and self-managementClear explanations so you know what is going on and what to do.
